# Reference email — built from the Northlight Films profile

Built from `examples/good/founder_led_boutique.md`.

```json
{
  "company": "Northlight Films",
  "subject": "A question about your location work",
  "first_email_angle": "A long-term partner, not a vendor",
  "body": "Hi Mara,\n\nI run color at Colorkaar and spend a lot of time looking at studios that shoot on location - your outdoor work has a consistent, restrained look that's harder to hold onto across a whole campaign than it looks.\n\nI'm curious how you're currently handling the grade on longer shoots, and whether that's something worth a short call about.\n\nUday",
  "most_distinctive_insight_used": "Founder still shoots",
  "conversation_starter_used": "Developing a consistent look on location",
  "evidence_strength": "Moderate",
  "reasoning": "first_email_angle carries colorkaar_angle through almost unchanged here because it was already the strongest hook. Built around the location-shooting consistency observation and the founder-to-founder framing. Kept short and low-pressure given evidence is Moderate, not Strong."
}
```

Notice what it doesn't do: no "I hope this finds you well," no listing of
Colorkaar's services, no mention of awards or named clients, no
exclamation points, no case study. It reads like one person who looked at
the actual work wrote a short note to another person.

Notice also that `first_email_angle` isn't always a rewrite of
`colorkaar_angle` — see `prompts/email.md`: when `most_distinctive_insight`
or `why_this_lead` is the stronger hook for a specific touchpoint, use that
instead. Here they happened to point the same direction, so it carried
through almost unchanged.
